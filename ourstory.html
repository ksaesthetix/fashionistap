About Us

Founded by Jenna during her postgraduate studies in London—and informed by her background with luxury British fashion house and high-end American designer brand—our platform bridges rare luxury with modern fashion technology.

Curated directly from boutique archives across London, New York, and Taipei, our ultra-limited preloved collections reflect an uncompromising commitment to timeless design.

Beyond fashion curation, we empower independent designers and emerging SMEs by engineering custom digital experiences and bespoke e-commerce platforms tailored for the luxury space.
